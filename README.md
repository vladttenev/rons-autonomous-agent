# RONS - Autonomous Market Participant

Most trading tools are designed to help humans make better decisions.
RONS is designed to make those decisions itself.
Built by Pons, RONS is an autonomous market participant that continuously researches markets, forms its own theses, publishes those theses publicly, and puts capital behind the ideas it believes in.
The system follows one simple process:
Research → Thesis → Post → Buy
That order is important.
RONS does not buy something first and explain the trade later. Before taking a position, it has to form a thesis and make that reasoning public.
The thesis comes first.
The trade comes second.
That means every position RONS takes starts with a visible explanation of what it believes, why it believes it, and what opportunity it thinks the market may be missing.

## How RONS Researches the Market
RONS is constantly looking for information that could matter.
It reads theses being published on @fomo, follows emerging narratives across the internet, tracks what traders are paying attention to, and searches for situations where market attention may not yet match the underlying opportunity.
But RONS is not built to simply copy what other people are saying.
Everything it reads is treated as an input.
RONS can agree with another trader’s thesis, disagree with it, investigate it further, or decide that the opportunity is already overcrowded.
The goal is to build an independent view.

## Thesis Before Trade
Once RONS identifies an opportunity it believes is worth acting on, it forms a clear thesis.
That thesis is then published publicly on Fomo before any buy takes place.
This creates a transparent sequence.
RONS researched the opportunity.
RONS formed a view.
RONS published that view.
Then RONS bought.
There is no waiting for the chart to move before explaining the idea.

## RONS Has Skin in the Game
This is what separates RONS from a normal research bot.
RONS does not simply generate market commentary.
It can actually participate in the market on Ethereum and Robinhood Chain.
If a thesis reaches a high enough level of conviction, RONS puts capital behind it and buys the coin itself, utilizing the PONS platform on Robinhood Chain for token launches and trading.

## Why Fomo Matters
Fomo is an important part of how RONS operates.
It acts as both a source of information and a public record of RONS’s reasoning.
RONS can read the theses being shared by other market participants, investigate those ideas independently, and use them as part of its broader research process.
But when RONS develops a thesis of its own, Fomo is also where that thesis becomes public.
